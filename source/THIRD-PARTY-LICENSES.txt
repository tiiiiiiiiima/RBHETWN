Additional imported resources — provenance notice for version 0.8
================================================================

Source: Bakugan Anime Style Format, Tabletop Simulator Steam Workshop
https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3412700725
The user supplied https://share.google/3LuahSzxTKkRx1G0t, which resolves
to this public Workshop page.

Contributors named on the inspected public page: MrZangetsu,
Low Taper Fade Rat, laval2400, Maxh1gh and rush808. The description
credits BakuganNewGame for card templates and MrZangetsu1997 for models,
and names Workshop 2480698294 as its model dependency.

30 previously absent ordinary species and 18 separate support/trap toys
were mapped from the source save. Cosmetic skins of already imported
species are excluded. Closed and open toys are distinct static models,
not recovered animated transformations or monster battle forms.
Original texture files remain unchanged; OBJ geometry is converted to
normalized GLB. Native attribute availability is preserved.

The additional art collection contains 636 distinct source card cells.
Atlas cells are cropped without resizing or recoloring. Seven source
card assets are MP4 videos; the shipped PNG is their first decoded frame,
and source animation is not played. Art collection entries do not by
themselves implement the printed effects.

One source table-board model/texture and one panorama are adapted to
the prototype field. Other furniture in the source is not claimed as
original Bakugan Wii or Bloxugan combat levels.

Detailed provenance, source URLs, SHA-256 values and transformations:
ASSET-SOURCES-0.8.md (docs/ in source), assets/imported/catalog.json,
assets/imported/source_cards.json and arenas/source_manifest.json.

The playable anime-duel subset contains 70 gates (60 attribute gates
and 10 commands) plus 20 abilities. It uses public descriptions from
https://bloxugan.info/json/cards.json?=v14 . Attribution and limitations
are recorded in BLOXUGAN-COMPARISON-0.8.md. This is a standalone fan
adaptation, not an official Roblox/Bloxugan client or a complete copy.
Resource integration does not change the original authorship or assign
a new license to the original games, brands or community resources.

Anime identity and support metadata — version 0.9
================================================

The new canonical_profiles.json records anime-character identity separately
from physical toy colours and the older Wii-mode prototype balance. Source
URLs, excerpts, confidence and per-attribute power variants accompany the
recorded facts. Public encyclopedia tables and available indexed wiki
excerpts support only the specific facts cited in each entry. Unknown or
ambiguous starting G-Power remains explicitly conditional; the catalog is
not attributed as a complete recovered table from the original games.

The trap_profiles.json records 17 traps and the distinct paired support
Shadow Wing. Printed support effects and source-format rules are credited
to the previously listed Anime Style Format Workshop contributors. The
source format's rule that traps lack their own G is recorded separately
from any anime G, and native model colours do not establish anime identity.
Battle deployment is a local adaptation. Metadata integration does not
alter authorship of the source card art, text, geometry or textures.

Fact-by-fact attribution and limitations: CANONICAL-PROFILES-0.9.md,
TRAPS-0.9.md and the two JSON manifests (docs/ in the source package).

Version 0.21 animated DS human avatars
=====================================
18 original DS brawler meshes/textures with native joint BCA0 animation,
converted using apicula to GLB. Indexed character names are from the
original mes_StoryCharaName.mes. Per-file source hashes and native clips
are retained in assets/ds/avatars/manifest.json inside the game PCK.
Original game asset authorship and rights remain with their original owners.
The PC tutorial, interface, input mappings and event dispatch are authored
for this prototype. Numeric voice groups are not asserted to be verified
character assignments. See ASSET-SOURCES-0.21.md for exact limits.
